Product Objective

Designed for Vehicle Interior Applications

Our Active Cabin Comfort Module is developed to support vehicle manufacturers across varied operating conditions and program requirements. The module is designed to coordinate interior system functions while maintaining consistency, reliability, and readiness for vehicle development timelines.

Design Decision Approach

Our new product development process combines customer requirements, engineering analysis, design reviews, and validation activities to support product design decisions. The development team evaluates design alternatives against program targets and coordinates updates through each stage of the vehicle development timeline. Artificial intelligence (AI) is used in this process.
