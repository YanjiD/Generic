Company Overview

About AuroraDrive Technologies

AuroraDrive Technologies Co., Ltd. is a supplier specializing in new product design and development for vehicle interior systems. The company works with customers to understand product requirements, evaluate design options, and support the development of customized components for new vehicle programs.

Product Design and Development

From Concept to Validated Design

Our team manages the full design and development cycle for vehicle interior components, translating customer requirements into manufacturable designs. Each program moves through structured stages of concept definition, detailed design, prototyping, and validation, with regular reviews to confirm that solutions meet technical, packaging, and cost targets.

Concept Definition

We work from customer requirements to define product concepts and evaluate feasible design directions.

Detailed Design

Selected concepts are developed into detailed component designs prepared for prototyping and tooling.

Prototyping

Prototypes are built to assess fit, packaging, and performance ahead of production commitment.

Design Validation

Designs are reviewed and tested against program targets to confirm readiness for development.

Customer Collaboration

Working Closely with Customers

We work closely with customers throughout each program, aligning on requirements early and maintaining regular communication as designs evolve. Collaborative reviews help confirm that proposed solutions meet packaging, performance, and cost expectations before development proceeds, so that decisions are made together with each customer's engineering and program teams.

Engineering and Program Support

Support Across the Program Timeline

Our engineering team supports customers across the development timeline, coordinating design updates, documentation, and change management. We align our activities with each vehicle program's milestones and provide continuity through the following phases:

  • Requirements alignment and design coordination
  • Documentation and engineering change management
  • Sourcing, tooling, and pre-production support
  • Program milestone and schedule alignment
